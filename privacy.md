# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026**

Thank you for using **Nightstand Alarm and Reminders**. Your privacy is not an afterthought in this app — it's the design. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

**Short version: the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and keeps everything on your device.**

## No data collection

Nightstand Alarm and Reminders does **not** collect any personal data. It does not ask you to create an account, and no account is required to use any feature.

## No network connections

The app makes **no network connections**. It works fully offline. Because it never talks to any server, none of your information is ever transmitted anywhere.

## No third-party SDKs, ads, analytics, or trackers

The app contains **no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no analytics, and no trackers**. There is no code in the app whose purpose is to observe, profile, or report on your behavior.

## Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in the app — your **alarms, reminders, medications, journal entries, and history logs** — is stored **only on your device**.

## Optional backups you control

The app offers optional backups so you don't lose your data:

- **iCloud Drive backups** are written to **your own iCloud account and iCloud Drive**.
- **Exported files** are JSON files that **you** export to a location **you** choose.

In both cases, these backups belong to you and stay within your control. **The developer never receives, stores, or has access to any of your data** — not your on-device data and not your backups.

## No account required

There is no sign-up and no login. You can use every feature of the app without providing any identifying information.

## System integrations

By design, the app does **not** use Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, App Intents, or activity donations. It does not share your alarms, reminders, or medications with other parts of the operating system. The app does use iOS notifications, because notifications are the only way it can alert you for alarms, reminders, and medication doses; you control whether notifications are allowed.

## Children's privacy

Nightstand Alarm and Reminders is safe for all ages and is rated **4+**. Because the app collects no personal data and makes no network connections, it does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
