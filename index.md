# Nightstand Alarm Clock

**A bedside display designed for nighttime use — power-efficient, sleep-friendly, and always readable.**

Nightstand Alarm Clock turns your iPhone into a calm, glare-free bedside clock and puts your alarms, reminders, medications, and a quick night journal in one place. It runs fully on your device — no account, no network, no tracking — so you get **peaceful, power-friendly nights** without giving anything away.

It's a **free iPhone app**.

## Quick Summary

A quick, scannable look at what the app does.

### Bedside Clock (Night Mode)
- Full-screen clock: large dim text on a black background, made for a dark room.
- The time slowly **drifts** across the screen to prevent OLED burn-in.
- Status bar and home indicator are hidden for a clean, distraction-free face.
- Tap or long-press to reveal controls; they auto-hide after about 6 seconds.
- Works in any orientation and re-centers when you rotate the phone.
- Jot a quick journal note straight from the dark clock.

### Clock Customization
- Show/hide seconds, 12- or 24-hour time, show/hide the date.
- Brightness from 15% to 100%.
- 12 text colors ranked by OLED power use (deep red is the default and easiest on the battery; blue uses the most power and is the most sleep-disrupting), plus a custom color picker.
- 18 font families, size 60–220 pt, weight and italic, and drift tuning — with a live preview and a one-tap **Reset to lowest power**.

### Alarms
- Time, label, and flexible schedules: Once, Daily, Weekly by weekday, Monthly by day-of-month, or Custom specific dates.
- 8 gentle built-in tones (for example, "Dawn" rises slowly) with preview.
- Full-screen ringing alert with Stop and Snooze; volume follows the device ringer.
- Enable/disable each alarm; swipe to delete.

### Reminders
- Three ways to schedule: **Once**, **Repeats** (calendar rules), or a **Sequence** of specific dates.
- Each reminder is a plain nudge or an **accountable task** with a deadline, expiry, and optional evidence.
- Group related reminders into **Plans** (like "Knee recovery"), with a month calendar, a due list, and adherence reports.
- Color coding, 8 built-in tones plus custom audio, and "Ring until stopped" escalation for critical items.

### Medications
- Dose-focused reminders with frequency shortcuts and an on-time **dose window** instead of a single instant.
- Mark taken (records the exact time), Skip, or Snooze each dose.
- A floating dose **bubble** drifts over the app when a dose is due — tap it to jump straight there.
- Adherence chart and a 4-letter color tag per medication. Dose alerts break through Focus and Do Not Disturb.

### Journal
- Quick bedside notes grouped by night, newest first.
- Capture from Night Mode without a Face ID prompt.
- Full-text search that shows the matching line; edit or delete anytime.

### Privacy
- Optionally anonymize medication names on the lock screen.
- No Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, App Intents, or activity donations — everything stays on-device.

## Privacy at a glance

- **Fully offline.** The app makes no network connections.
- **No account.** Nothing to sign up for.
- **No tracking, ads, or analytics.** There are no third-party SDKs.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Optional backups go to *your own* iCloud Drive or a JSON file *you* export and control. The developer never receives any of it.
